How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × M × G × A × C. M is one market multiple for the whole board: the median mcap/TVL across the snapshot's own tokenized peers (). G is growth: 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%. A is an age premium: 1 + 0.12 per year the protocol has run with no token, capped at 4 years. C is chain breadth: 1 + 0.06 per extra chain, capped at 10. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

What the snapshot does not carry: it holds ten fields per protocol and no fees, no revenue, no volume and no user counts. A revenue-multiple model is impossible from this snapshot, so this radar does not claim one — it uses TVL, 7-day growth, age and chain breadth, and says so.

Who is ranked: every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist (TVL ≥ $50M, no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product) plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the floor. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart in the Live section below — the board shows the market's own value for them, never a model's.

Implied values are this entry's own claim, never a prediction of whether or when a token is issued. Farm plans are six concrete steps with costs and times, derived from each protocol's category, chains, TVL and age.
